A PRACTICAL USER GUIDE

How to use Equity Atlas

This is a local research and paper-trading app for NSE shares. It checks completed daily candles, explains potential setups, and estimates risk. It does not place orders or guarantee a return. Before the regular market closes at 15:30 India time, the latest completed candle will usually be from the previous trading session.

Start with this workflow

  1. Check the connection. The header should say “Local service connected.” Keep the local server running. If it is unavailable, start run_screener.bat from the project folder, then refresh this page.
  2. Research one stock first. On Opportunities, type an NSE symbol such as DIXON or HINDCOPPER and choose Analyze stock. This shows trend, returns, volatility and available patterns even when there is no trade setup.
  3. Build a full shortlist. Open EMA screener, choose F&O or All NSE, and press Scan Now. Start with verified uptrend and Actionable only. An empty shortlist means no setup passed these rules in the requested window.
  4. Inspect the reasons. Open Evidence & limitations and View chart. To study rejected signals, turn off Actionable only. This reveals the watchlist; it does not remove the underlying entry blockers.
  5. Plan a paper risk budget. Choose Plan risk. Check entry, stop, target, capital and costs before Calculate paper size. The calculation estimates whole-share quantity without leverage.
  6. Track and review. Paper track records a hypothetical entry at the displayed entry reference. Open Paper journal and Refresh completed outcomes on later sessions. Use Backtests to inspect historical behavior and sample size before trusting any rule.

1 · Opportunities and stock research

The quick scan checks the first 60 symbols in the selected registry. It is a preview of that universe, not a scan of every share. Use EMA screener for full coverage.

  • Valuation confluence: an actionable technical setup with at least 5% estimated fair-value headroom and enough model evidence.
  • Technical shortlist: actionable momentum when qualifying valuation evidence is unavailable.
  • Stock lookup: price date, moving-average trend, RSI, ATR, volume, 5/20/60/120-session returns, available 52-week range, and EMA or reversal setups.
  • Coverage: how many symbols were requested and which had unavailable history. Missing data does not prove the stock has no setup.

2 · EMA screener

A signal needs a close crossing above EMA9, then a following completed candle with a higher close and higher high. A verified uptrend needs price above SMA50 and SMA200, SMA50 above SMA200 and rising, plus a positive 60-session slope.

  • Breakout window: how many trading sessions back to search for confirmation.
  • Trend filter: broaden it to inspect sideways or falling stocks; weaker signals remain watch-only.
  • Actionable only: show candidates passing freshness, liquidity, confirmation volume, ATR extension, resistance and estimated reward/risk rules.
  • Nifty / relative strength: optional extra requirements for an advancing benchmark or a stock outperforming Nifty over matched 60-session dates.
  • Min score, sort and search: focus the returned rows. Export CSV saves the currently filtered research results.

Setup target belongs to the original signal entry and stays fixed. New entry target is calculated from the latest completed close. These can differ because the stock has moved.

3 · Support reversals

Reversals search for independently confirmed support retests, including double bottoms and support shelves. Being close to a low is a reason to watch, not automatically a buy.

  • Support floor: a tested base, not a guaranteed minimum price.
  • First resistance: the nearest confirmed overhead barrier. A farther peak or fair value cannot replace a closer barrier to inflate upside.
  • Min upside: minimum chart distance to that first resistance. Raising it reduces candidates; it does not make future returns more certain.
  • Pattern and status filters: compare confirmed reversals with unconfirmed support watches. Actionable entries also need volume, intact support and adequate reward/risk.
  • Structural stop: below the floor with an ATR buffer. A tracked reversal uses an intraday PRICE stop, rather than the EMA close-exit rule.

4 · Valuation

Enter a symbol and choose the earliest fiscal year to include. Annual profit, annual sales and quarterly trailing-twelve-month models relate fundamentals to past prices. Only sufficiently supported models contribute to the composite estimate.

  • Confidence and fundamentals date: inspect age, sample counts, exclusions and model disagreement before reading the estimated upside.
  • Model tabs: inspect operating profit, sales and TTM charts, equations and period selectors.
  • Time ranges and bands: change the displayed price history and model-estimate overlays.
  • Annual and quarterly tables: examine the actual observations behind the calculations.

R² measures historical fit. It is not a chance of winning a trade. Historical fair-value lines are retrospective fits and lack publication-date evidence; they cannot be treated as values known on those historical dates. Valuation can be unavailable while the price chart remains usable.

5 · Risk planner and paper journal

Plan risk uses the selected setup to fill entry, stop and target. Enter your research capital and risk percentage. For example, ₹100,000 capital and 1% risk creates a ₹1,000 estimated loss budget. Entry ₹100, stop ₹95, target ₹110 and 30 bps round-trip costs gives 188 whole shares and an estimated ₹996.40 loss.

  • Quantity: constrained by both available cash and estimated loss budget.
  • Net reward/risk: estimated gain after costs divided by estimated loss. Overnight gaps can exceed the stop-based estimate.
  • Paper track: records a hypothetical entry; no brokerage order is sent. A new entry has its own fixed target.
  • Journal filters: separate active, pending and closed entries. Refresh outcomes evaluates completed bars after entry, not highs that occurred before the recorded entry.
  • Remove entry: removes that saved entry; another entry in the same stock is preserved. Clear list removes the entire journal after confirmation.

6 · Backtests

Choose Strategy first: the original close-reference duration study, next-open duration with one position per stock, or confirmed EMA with an EMA stop. Duration defaults to a 3% target and 90 trading sessions, roughly four calendar months. Select the basket, years, costs and optional filters, then run the ticker progress.

  • Execution: close-reference duration permits overlapping signals; next-open duration models a later fill and one position per stock. Both duration modes have no stop. Confirmed EMA uses its close invalidation and following-open exit.
  • Mature target rate: entries with the entire holding horizon available, including early winners. Observed hits / all entries also includes recent unresolved entries. Compare the complete counts.
  • Win rate: percentage of closed trades profitable after modeled costs. Target-hit rate is separate; a time exit can be profitable without hitting the target.
  • Profit factor and expectancy: compare total profits with losses and average net result per closed trade.
  • Sample size and interval: few trades produce weak evidence, even with a high observed win rate.
  • Tables and exports: inspect per-stock and individual outcomes, pending exits and data gaps. Duration CSV exports every matching outcome; full-run JSON preserves all entries, settings and counts. A winner-only file cannot establish accuracy.

Duration shows target time, worst adverse moves and simultaneous exposure, with no overlapping profit compounding. The confirmed-EMA curve uses fixed-allocation realized P&L and excludes open drawdowns. The optional cached-current-FV subset is retrospective. Historical valuation comparison remains unverified without publication-dated values.

Evidence score / 100Ranks rule evidence, not a calibrated profit probability. Always read the blockers; even a high score can remain watch-only.
EMA9 / SMA50 / SMA200Averages of closing prices over short, medium and long horizons. Price and slope relationships describe the current trend.
ATR14Recent typical daily price range in rupees. Higher ATR means more price movement and a wider uncertainty around stop estimates.
RSI14Momentum on a 0–100 scale. A high value can indicate strong momentum or extension; it is not a standalone buy signal.
Confirmation volumeSignal-day volume divided by its prior 20-session average. 1.5× means 50% above that average. Missing or mostly zero volume weakens the evidence.
Relative strengthStock return minus Nifty return over matching 60-session endpoints. +3% means three percentage points of outperformance.

NEARING TARGETThe original target is close. Remaining reward may be too small for a new entry.
TARGET HIT / STOPPED OUT / TIME EXITThe original setup or journal entry has a terminal outcome. A later recovery does not reactivate that old trade. Net profit depends on the exit and costs.
STOP PENDING / TIME EXIT PENDINGA completed-close exit decision exists, but the next session's execution has not yet been observed. It is not a finalized profit or loss.
Watchlist / entry blockedSome entry checks failed or evidence is unavailable. View the chart and reasons, rather than treating the row as a recommendation.

Troubleshooting charts and data

Reading scan and backtest progress

Bulk scans show the local worker's current phase, completed symbols, total symbols and current ticker. The bar uses reported progress; it stays animated without a percentage while the worker prepares its universe. Data downloads, valuation checks and historical simulation can take different amounts of time. Unavailable symbols are counted separately from candidates that passed the rules. Wait for the completed results before comparing scans.

“Cannot reach the local service” or “Failed to fetch”

The browser could not complete its request to the app. Keep the local server running, start run_screener.bat if needed, then refresh the page and Retry chart. The connection badge reflects the last connection check, not a promise that the process is still running.

“Insufficient completed daily history”

This can mean a provider failed to download prices, a new listing has little history, or too few valid completed bars were received. Use a valid NSE symbol, retry, and read the returned data-coverage message. It does not mean a long-established stock necessarily has no history. Intraday candles are intentionally excluded from confirmed daily signals.

Chart library or valuation unavailable

Price, trend and level values can still be useful without valuation. If chart rendering itself is unavailable, retry the chart and read its error. A missing fundamental model should not prevent the basic price chart from opening.

No actionable candidates

Check universe, breakout window, trend and optional Nifty filters. Turn off Actionable only to inspect why candidates were rejected. Already-hit targets, stopped setups, insufficient history, low volume, extension or nearby resistance can all block an entry. Broadening display filters does not create reliable trade evidence.

How to use the charts

Hover over a candle or point for values. Drag to zoom a region, use the date-range controls where shown, and double-click to reset the view. The labels identify price, moving averages, original target, structural support or stop reference. A support line is an estimated level, not guaranteed protection.
